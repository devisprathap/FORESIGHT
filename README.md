#  PROJECT FORESIGHT

## Demand Forecasting & Inventory Intelligence

Project FORESIGHT is a data-driven dashboard developed to analyze sales,
understand customer demand, forecast future demand, monitor inventory health,
identify stock risks, and provide business recommendations.

---

##  Objectives

- Analyze historical sales data
- Understand customer demand
- Analyze product performance
- Monitor inventory health
- Identify stockout and overstock risks
- Analyze promotions
- Identify seasonal patterns
- Forecast future demand
- Provide business recommendations
- Build an interactive Streamlit dashboard

---

##  Dashboard Pages

The dashboard contains:

1. Company Overview
2. Sales Performance
3. Customer Demand
4. Product Performance
5. Inventory Health
6. Stock Risk
7. Promotion Analysis
8. Seasonality
9. Forecast
10. Executive Summary
11. Recommendation
